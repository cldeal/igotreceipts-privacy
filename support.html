Questions, problems, or feedback — here's how to reach us and answers to the common ones.

We read every message. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if you're reporting a problem.

Why don't I see written bullets after recording?

The written summaries are produced by Apple Intelligence, which runs on your device. It's available on iPhone 15 Pro and later, on iOS 26 or newer, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. On an unsupported device the app still records and transcribes your notes — it just can't rewrite them into bullets. The app tells you when that's the case.

Where are my notes stored? Can you see them?

Everything stays on your iPhone. Your voice is transcribed on-device, your notes are rewritten on-device, and nothing is uploaded. We never receive your data and have no way to see it. See the Privacy Policy for details.

What does Receipts Pro include?

Daily capture, review bullets, history, your streak, and the widget are free forever. Pro unlocks two things: STAR interview stories assembled from a project's notes, and a weekly update you can send your manager. Both come with a one-week free trial.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → I Got Receipts! → Cancel. If you cancel during the free trial, you won't be charged.

How do I delete my data?

Swipe to delete individual receipts, stories, or weekly updates inside the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Because we never receive your data, there's nothing to request from us.
